Drafting Benefit Dashboard

Power as a fraction of each rider's own average lead power, by position and by rider in front. Lower ratio = more drafting benefit.

Data source & view

Built-in datasets are Nilai Q, HongKong Q, HongKong R1, HongKong F and Nilai R1 from Drafting.xlsx. Upload another xlsx with the same column layout (rider names in cols 1–4, powers in power 1–4, optional Start/Finish) to replace. Overall averages each metric across the per-sheet results.

Average lead power per rider

Each rider's baseline = simple mean of their power while in position 1 (per sheet; in Overall, averaged across sheets that had them lead).

Benefit by position — overall

Mean ratio of power in position ÷ own average lead power. Position 1 is by definition ~1.00.

Benefit by position — per rider

Same metric, broken down for each rider individually.

Drafting matrix — by rider in front

Rows = drafting rider. Columns = rider directly ahead (position N−1). Cell = mean power ratio of the drafting rider when sitting behind that rider, regardless of position. Lower = bigger draft.

Draft given by leader — by leader's position

Rows = the rider in front. Columns = the position that rider is in (so the follower is one position back). Cell = mean power ratio of whichever rider was directly behind them, expressed against that follower's own average lead power. Lower = the leader gave a bigger draft.
